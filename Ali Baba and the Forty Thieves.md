# Ali Baba and the Forty Thieves

Long ago, in a small village surrounded by rocky mountains, there lived a poor woodcutter named Ali Baba. Every day, he went into the forest to collect fallen branches and firewood. He would then take them home so that his wife, Morgiana, could use them for cooking.

Ali Baba was a simple and hardworking man. He did not have much money, but he was honest and content with what he had.

One morning, Ali Baba went deeper into the forest than usual. He was busy collecting dry branches when he suddenly heard a strange sound.

**CLATTER! CLATTER! CLATTER!**

The sound of horses' hooves echoed through the mountains.

Ali Baba froze.

Someone was coming.

He quickly gathered his things, climbed up a nearby tree, and hid among the thick leaves.

Soon, a large group of horsemen appeared on the rocky path. Ali Baba carefully counted them as they passed.

“One... two... three... four...”

He kept counting.

There were **forty men**.

They looked rough and dangerous, and every one of them carried a weapon. Their horses were loaded with large bags that seemed extremely heavy.

Ali Baba watched silently.

“These must be thieves,” he thought. “And those bags must be filled with stolen treasure!”

The forty men stopped in front of a huge rocky cliff. Their leader climbed down from his horse and walked towards the rock.

Ali Baba could not understand what they were doing.

Then the leader raised his voice and shouted:

**“Khullja Sim Sim!”**

To Ali Baba's amazement, a hidden door suddenly appeared in the cliff and swung open.

The thieves carried their bags inside.

Once everyone had entered, the leader turned towards the rock and called out:

**“Sim Sim Bandhojao!”**

The magical door immediately closed.

Ali Baba could hardly believe his eyes.

“A secret cave... and magic words!” he whispered.

He stayed hidden in the tree until the thieves finally came back out. He counted all forty of them and waited until their horses had disappeared into the distance.

When he was certain that the thieves were gone, Ali Baba climbed down.

He stood in front of the rocky cliff.

His heart was pounding.

“Could the magic words really work for me?”

He took a deep breath and said:

**“Khullja Sim Sim!”**

WHOOSH!

The secret door opened.

Ali Baba stepped inside.

The door closed.

A staircase led deep into the mountain. Small oil lamps lit the way. He slowly walked down the steps until he reached the bottom.

Then he stopped.

His mouth fell open.

The cave was filled with treasure.

There were mountains of gold coins, sparkling diamonds, glittering rubies, shining emeralds, and countless other precious jewels.

Ali Baba had never seen anything like it.

But he was not greedy.

He did not try to take everything.

Instead, he picked up just **one small gold coin**.

“That is enough,” he said. “This will help my family.”

He hurried back towards the entrance.

At the door, he remembered the words the thief had used.

Ali Baba called out:

**“Khullja Sim Sim!”**

The door opened.

He quickly stepped outside and turned around.

**“Sim Sim Bandhojao!”**

The magical door closed.

Ali Baba returned home with his little gold coin.

He told Morgiana about the incredible cave and warned her to keep the secret.

Unfortunately, someone else had seen what happened.

Ali Baba's brother, Kassim, had noticed him leaving the mountains. Kassim was wealthy, but he was extremely greedy.

When Ali Baba told him about the cave, Kassim could think of only one thing.

“Treasure!”

The next morning, Kassim rushed to the cave with several large bags.

He stood before the rock and shouted:

**“Khullja Sim Sim!”**

The door opened.

Kassim rushed inside.

The door closed.

He stared at the treasure with wide eyes.

“Gold! Jewels! Diamonds!”

He began filling his bags.

One bag became full.

Then another.

And another.

Kassim became so excited that he forgot the magic words.

When he finally decided to leave, he ran to the entrance.

He stood in front of the door and confidently shouted:

**“Khullja Jim Jim!”**

Nothing happened.

Kassim blinked.

“Huh?”

He tried again.

**“Khullja Kim Kim!”**

Nothing.

**“Khullja Dim Dim!”**

Still nothing.

Kassim panicked.

“What were those words?!”

He tried every phrase he could remember.

But the door remained closed.

Then he heard voices outside.

The forty thieves had returned.

The door opened.

The thieves discovered Kassim inside their secret cave with their treasure.

Kassim begged for mercy, but the thieves were furious.

The greedy man never returned home.

When Kassim's wife realized that something was wrong, she went to Ali Baba for help.

Ali Baba immediately understood that his brother had gone back to the cave.

He hurried to the mountains and found Kassim.

With a heavy heart, Ali Baba brought his brother home so that the family could give him a proper burial.

But the story did not end there.

When the thieves returned to their cave, they noticed that Kassim's body was gone.

Their leader became suspicious.

“Someone else knows about our secret cave!”

He followed the tracks from the mountains and eventually discovered Ali Baba's house.

The leader came up with a plan.

The next day, he would disguise himself as an olive-oil merchant. He would arrive at Ali Baba's house with a large cart carrying dozens of enormous oil jars.

But there was a secret inside those jars.

Hidden inside each one was a thief.

The leader planned to wait until nightfall, then release his men and attack Ali Baba.

The thieves arrived at Ali Baba's house the following day.

The leader knocked on the door.

Ali Baba opened it.

“Who are you?”

“I am an olive-oil merchant,” the leader replied. “My journey has been long. Would you allow me to spend the night here?”

Ali Baba agreed.

He had no idea that danger had arrived at his doorstep.

But Morgiana noticed something strange about the jars.

She quietly walked around the cart and listened.

From one of the jars came a faint whisper.

“Is it time?”

Morgiana immediately understood.

“These aren't oil jars,” she thought. “There are men hiding inside!”

She rushed back into the house and told Ali Baba.

Together, they came up with a clever plan.

Morgiana dealt with the thieves one by one, preventing them from escaping their hiding places. By the time the leader realized something had gone terribly wrong, his entire plan had failed.

Morgiana then called for the village guards.

The guards arrived and captured the thieves.

The dangerous gang that had terrorized travelers and villages for years was finally defeated.

The villagers recovered the stolen treasure, and much of it was returned to the people who had originally lost it.

Some treasure remained.

The village Sheikh decided to give a portion of it to Ali Baba and Morgiana as a reward for their courage and cleverness.

Ali Baba never became greedy.

He remembered what had happened to his brother.

From that day forward, he used his good fortune wisely and lived peacefully with Morgiana.

And somewhere deep in the mountains, behind a magical door hidden inside a rocky cliff, the secret words were never forgotten:

**“Khullja Sim Sim!”**
